New client `@epilot/journey-session-client` and `epilot.journeySession` in `@epilot/sdk` (`@epilot/sdk/journey-session`) for the Journey Session API, which runs journeys without a screen for chats, AI agents and APIs: start, read, answer, continue, go back, submit, hand off to the web, snapshots, and the records and options of a block, for logged-in portal customers and for public visitors.
